Curve: approximate BER vs SNR for each scheme (Gray-coded QAM, AWGN-equivalent). Marker = current operating point. Fixed 64-QAM would sit on the steep right-hand curve and fail badly the moment SNR drops — that gap is the adaptive scheme's whole justification.

Extra realism modelled

• Path loss grows with distance from the tower (log-distance exponent n, higher for car/train due to street clutter).
• Shadowing — random log-normal fluctuation (buildings, foliage) layered on top of fading, resampled continuously.
• Multipath count scales delay spread — more reflectors (buildings, tunnels) → larger delay spread → more inter-symbol interference risk, which is why OFDM/cyclic-prefix is used at high SNR.
• Coherence time T_c ≈ 0.423/f_d shown per scenario — at train speeds it drops sharply, meaning the channel estimate can go stale between adaptation decisions unless updates are fast enough.
